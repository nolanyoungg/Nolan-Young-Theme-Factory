Featured case study

A service website rebuilt around clearer choices.

This representative case study shows the kind of work the theme supports: a scattered service site became a more direct system of service pages, proof points, content prompts, and inquiry paths.

Challenge
Visitors could not quickly compare services or understand the next step.
Solution
Circuit Commerce Studio-style templates emphasized services, process, support, and contact flow.
Outcome
The site became easier to scan, maintain, and extend without claiming unsupported performance numbers.

Before and after

From scattered pages to a guided website system.

Before

  • Services described in disconnected page blocks.
  • Navigation relied on visitors guessing what mattered.
  • Forms lacked context for the inquiry type.

After

  • Service paths, work examples, and FAQs support decisions.
  • Header panels expose the most important destinations.
  • Contact and service forms capture useful context.

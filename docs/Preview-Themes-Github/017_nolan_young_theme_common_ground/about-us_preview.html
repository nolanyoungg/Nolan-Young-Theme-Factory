The studio / Common Ground

A practice of
paying attention.

We believe a good building makes everyday life feel a little easier. It holds the light, welcomes change, and belongs to its surroundings.

Thoughtful by nature

Start with what
is already good.

A well-placed window. A wall with a useful history. A garden that catches the afternoon sun. These are the starting points for our work.

Common Ground is an imagined independent practice for homeowners and small cultural organizations. Our approach brings the same care to a kitchen threshold as to a shared reading room: understand the daily routines, ask what matters, and give each decision a clear purpose.

We favor direct conversations, legible drawings, and materials that can be maintained. The ambition is quiet: places that earn their keep, year after year.

Our principles

Three things we return to.

01 / Enough

Make space count.

Before adding floor area, look at how the existing rooms connect. A simpler route or a shared threshold can unlock more than an extension.

02 / Endurance

Think beyond the finish.

Choose for touch, repair, and daily wear. A material should look at home now and remain useful as lives and needs change.

03 / Belonging

Look beyond the boundary.

Consider neighbors, streets, and shared edges. Even a modest project can make its little part of the world more welcoming.

Working together

A clear path, taken together.

  1. 01

    Listen closely

    We discuss what works, what gets in the way, and what you can realistically invest. Your brief begins with everyday life.

  2. 02

    Explore carefully

    We compare a small number of meaningful options, explaining the tradeoffs in space, cost, light, and complexity.

  3. 03

    Resolve together

    We develop the preferred direction in stages, with clear decisions and agreed responsibilities before moving forward.

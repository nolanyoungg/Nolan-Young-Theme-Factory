# Customization

Customize global colors, spacing, typography, and component styling in `src/scss/`. The compiled files in `assets/css/bundle.css` and `assets/js/bundle.js` are generated outputs and should keep their required names.

Business copy lives in templates and helper data arrays. Service, article, and work-card data is centralized in `inc/helpers.php`; page-specific narrative lives in `front-page.php`, `page-templates/`, and `template-parts/`.

Use only approved local media from `assets/images/asset-manifest.json` for stock-photo content. SVG files in `assets/icons/` are reserved for local interface marks and small UI details.

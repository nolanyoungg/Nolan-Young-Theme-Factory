Ask about a weekend box ↗

Demo only. No ordering, payment or reservation happens here.

Simple ingredients.
Nothing mysterious.

House loaf: wheat flour, water, sourdough starter (wheat flour, water), salt. Buns: wheat flour, milk, butter, eggs, honey, yeast and salt. Honey butter: butter, honey and salt.

What about allergies and substitutions?

The box contains wheat, milk and eggs. The shared bakery handles sesame and nuts. This sample offering is not suitable for people requiring an allergen-free environment. Substitutions would need to be discussed before a real order.

How should I store everything?

Keep the loaf wrapped at room temperature and freeze slices you won’t eat soon. Refrigerate the honey butter and use within three days. Buns are best on the day, gently warmed.

Can I order more than one box?

For larger gatherings, use the catering inquiry layout to explore your plans. It is a static demo and does not send a request.

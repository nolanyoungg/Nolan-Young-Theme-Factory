THE RHYTHM OF THE DAY

One step.
Then another.

Timings are illustrative and include breaks. Ground conditions, weather and the group’s pace would shape any real itinerary.

  1. 08:30

    Meet & find our feet

    Introductions, a kit check and a conversation about the route, pace and turnaround options.

  2. 09:00

    A steady climb

    Take the ascent in stages, pausing to regroup and watch the view open up.

  3. 12:00

    Lunch with a horizon

    Find a sheltered place for a packed lunch before following the broad ridge.

  4. 15:30

    Return to the trailhead

    Descend at a comfortable pace, with time for a final stretch and a look back.

  • Route adjustments around conditions and group comfort

What you would bring.

  • Travel to the agreed meeting point
  • Your lunch, water and personal walking kit
  • Experience of longer hill walks and sustained ascent

A few things to know

Do I need walking experience?

The gentle day walks are a good starting point. Ridge routes suit regular hill walkers. Share your experience and any access needs before choosing a route.

What if the weather changes?

The sample plan includes a sheltered alternative. In real trip planning, route changes, postponements and turnaround decisions would be discussed before departure.

Can I come on my own?

Yes, the small-group concept is designed with solo walkers in mind. Private guiding is another option for a day shaped around your own pace.

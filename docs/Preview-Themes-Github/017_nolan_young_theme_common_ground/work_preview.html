01 / Renovation study

Courtyard House

A familiar home, rearranged around a pocket of daylight. The proposal finds more room in the plan before asking more of the site.

Setting
A quiet inner suburb
Brief
Renovation with a small rear addition
Status
Fictional concept

The question

How could a deep, narrow house feel connected to its garden without replacing the spaces that give it character? The imagined owners wanted a shared kitchen and a quiet place to work.

The response

Retain the front rooms, bring circulation beside a small courtyard, and gather cooking and eating beneath one simple roof. Existing doors and sound timber would be surveyed for reuse.

02 / Compact home study

Little Gable

A new home for a small plot at a village edge. The concept replaces spare corridors with connected rooms and uses a sheltered threshold to extend everyday life outdoors.

A flexible ground-floor room could serve as a workspace, guest room, or future bedroom. A central service zone keeps the remaining plan clear, while deep window reveals offer places to sit and pause.

The material idea is deliberately limited: a durable outer layer, a straightforward timber interior, and parts that can be reached for repair.

Setting
A village edge
Brief
A small, adaptable new home
Status
Fictional concept
Guiding idea
Less circulation, more living

03 / Civic interior study

The Reading Room

An ordinary shopfront becomes an open invitation: a place to read, meet a neighbor, or stay a little longer.

Illustrative architectural reference · Pierre Châtel-Innocenti / Unsplash

Setting
A neighborhood high street
Brief
A shared reading and meeting space
Status
Fictional concept

A room that can change

Low shelves would define quieter edges while movable tables keep the center open. A reading session in the morning could give way to a workshop in the afternoon.

Care behind the scenes

The proposal reserves proper space for storage, a clear entrance, and an easy-to-find welcome desk. Acoustic finishes and controllable light support different ways of using the room.

Good places begin with a conversation.

A room to rethink. A small plot. An idea for your neighborhood.

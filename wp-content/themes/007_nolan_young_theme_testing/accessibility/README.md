# Accessibility

At Northstar Codeworks, we are committed to creating accessible and inclusive digital experiences for all users. Our team follows industry best practices and standards to ensure that our custom web applications, internal tools, API integrations, and automation systems are usable by people of all abilities.

### Key Accessibility Features:

- **Semantic HTML**: We use proper HTML tags and structure to provide meaningful context for screen readers and assistive technologies.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: All interactive elements on our websites can be accessed and navigated using keyboard shortcuts alone, enhancing usability for users with motor impairments.
- **Sufficient Contrast**: Text and background colors have a high contrast ratio to ensure readability for users with visual impairments.
- **Responsive Design**: Our layouts are fully responsive, adapting seamlessly to different devices and screen sizes without compromising accessibility.
- **Alt Text for Images**: All images include descriptive alt text to convey the purpose or content of the image to visually impaired users.
- **Aria Labels and Roles**: We use ARIA labels and roles where necessary to enhance the accessibility of complex components like navigation menus and forms.

### Accessibility Standards:

We adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA standards to ensure our digital products meet the highest level of accessibility. This includes considerations for perceptibility, operability, understandability, and robustness.

By prioritizing accessibility in all aspects of development, Northstar Codeworks aims to create software solutions that are not only technically sophisticated but also inclusive and user-friendly.

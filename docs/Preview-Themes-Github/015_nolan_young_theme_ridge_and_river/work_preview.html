Field notes / Imagined days outside

Routes with room to roam.

Three sample itineraries to help you picture a day. These are fictional route concepts, not records of completed trips or directions to follow on the ground.

NOTE 01 / THE HIGH LINE

A long ridge.
A wide-open day.

Climb through the morning, settle into the skyline and leave time for a sheltered lunch. A sustained day for regular hill walkers.

NOTE 02 / THE WATER LINE

A riverside ramble.

A gentle 6 km loop in the sample plan. Begin on a meadow path, stop for a flask of tea and return along a woodland edge. Allow three hours, including plenty of pauses.

Look out for
Changing light on the water and the quiet between footfalls.
Plan around
Wet ground, seasonal path closures and considerate use of shared trails.
Explore day walks ↗

NOTE 03 / THE LEARNING LINE

Finding a bearing.

A 5 km teaching loop in the sample plan. Match the contours to the hillside, identify useful landmarks and practise a simple bearing in an unhurried four-hour session.

Look out for
The small features that turn a paper map into a recognisable place.
Plan around
Good visibility, a suitable practice area and time for questions.

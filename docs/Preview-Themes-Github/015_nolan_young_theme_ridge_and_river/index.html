02 / Find your pace

There’s no prize
for the fastest.

Pick a day that feels right for you. Distance is only part of the story; terrain, ascent and weather matter too.

Let’s talk about the right fit ↗
Ⅰ

Easy does it

Gentle paths, unhurried breaks and around 2–3 hours on your feet. Expect some uneven ground.

Ⅱ

A little more uphill

Longer climbs and rougher trails over 4–5 hours. Best if you already enjoy a regular countryside walk.

Ⅲ

The bigger day

Sustained ascent, exposed ground and up to 7 hours out. A comfortable choice for experienced hill walkers.

A few things to know

Do I need walking experience?

The gentle day walks are a good starting point. Ridge routes suit regular hill walkers. Share your experience and any access needs before choosing a route.

What if the weather changes?

The sample plan includes a sheltered alternative. In real trip planning, route changes, postponements and turnaround decisions would be discussed before departure.

Can I come on my own?

Yes, the small-group concept is designed with solo walkers in mind. Private guiding is another option for a day shaped around your own pace.

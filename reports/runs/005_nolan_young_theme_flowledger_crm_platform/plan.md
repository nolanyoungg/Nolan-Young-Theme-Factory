### Creative Execution Brief for FlowLedger CRM Website

**Page Map:**
1. Home Page
2. About Page
3. Services Overview Page
   - Pipeline Management
   - Client Records
   - Form Intake and Admin Review
   - Task and Follow-Up Automation
   - Customer Health Dashboards
   - Reporting and Export Tools
4. Work / Customer Stories Page
5. Resources / Blog Page
6. Contact Page

**Content Direction:**
- Clear product marketing with concrete workflow examples.
- Focus on features, use cases, implementation, and fit.
- Avoid vague startup filler and generic business growth claims.

**Design Direction:**
- Modern SaaS dashboard style with polished dark-mode hero.
- Crisp product panels, clear feature sections, strong contrast, refined sp
spacing.
- Local generated dashboard-style raster assets showing CRM pipeline boards
boards, customer timelines, account health cards, form-entry tables, task q
queues, admin export screens, and reporting charts.

**Interaction Direction:**
- Subtle hover states, polished dropdown navigation, lightweight scroll rev
reveal feel, animated hero/dashboard composition.
- Accessible reduced-motion behavior, no distracting motion.

**Image/Art Direction:**
- Local generated dashboard-style raster assets that feel like product UI v
visuals.

**Risks:**
- Misrepresentation of features or product clarity.
- Overloading content on any page.
- Poor usability on mobile devices.

**Execution Priorities:**
1. Ensure a complete and product-focused homepage.
2. Maintain strong visual consistency across the site.
3. Optimize for accessibility and responsive design.
4. Implement clear CTA strategies on all pages.
5. Focus on providing useful, actionable content.

This brief outlines the strategic execution plan for the FlowLedger CRM web
website, ensuring it effectively communicates the product's value while mai
maintaining a polished, professional appearance.

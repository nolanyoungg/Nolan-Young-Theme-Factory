Process

From first estimate to tidy closeout notes.

  1. Request estimate

    Share the property type, timing, services needed, and any useful photos.

  2. Walk the property

    The crew reviews access, turf, beds, slopes, cleanup volume, and seasonal priorities.

  3. Choose the plan

    Pick recurring route care, a one-time reset, bed refresh work, or a seasonal cleanup.

  4. Scheduled visits

    Visits are grouped by route, weather, and property notes so service stays predictable.

  5. Tidy closeout notes

    Edges, gates, beds, debris, and follow-up needs are checked before the visit wraps.

Privacy / Demo information

A clear view of this site.

Ridge & River is a fictional sample brand. This page describes the theme’s demonstration features.

Your sample inquiry

The inquiry form is a local demonstration. Its controls check the entries in your browser and display a notice. The theme does not send emails, transmit form entries, store submissions or accept payments. Please use invented details.

Local assets and privacy

The theme loads its own scripts, styles, icons and photograph. It includes no analytics, advertising tags, remote fonts or tracking services. Your hosting provider or WordPress installation may independently process technical request information.

Links and photography

Photographer and licence links lead to Unsplash. Visiting those websites is subject to their own policies. The photograph is illustrative stock, not evidence of trips operated by Ridge & River.

Before a real launch

A real operator would need to provide accurate business details, a privacy notice reflecting their hosting and services, and actual booking and cancellation terms. No bookings or guiding services are offered through this demonstration.

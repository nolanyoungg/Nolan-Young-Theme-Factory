The inquiry demo

Fields on the contact page are demonstration controls. The demo button shows an on-page explanation only. The theme does not send field contents to a server, save them in a database, email them, or store them in your browser. Please use sample information.

Cookies and third parties

The theme adds no analytics, tracking scripts, remote fonts or marketing cookies. WordPress, hosting services and any separately installed plugins may handle technical logs or cookies independently. A site operator must describe those services before using this theme for a real business.

Photographs and links

Approved stock photographs are served from this theme. Attribution links lead to Unsplash; visiting those links takes you to a third-party website with its own policies. The photographs do not depict products made by this fictional brand.

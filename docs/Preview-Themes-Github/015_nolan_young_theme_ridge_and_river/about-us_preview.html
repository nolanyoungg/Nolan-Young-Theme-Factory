Our story / Ridge & River

Less to prove. More to discover.

A small outdoor guiding and walking outfitter, imagined for people who would rather take their time than race the daylight.

A SIMPLE STARTING POINT

Outside is
for noticing.

Ridge & River began as a fictional idea around a familiar feeling: a good walk can change the shape of a whole week. It needn’t be a grand expedition. Sometimes a river path is enough.

Our sample trips are built around small groups, patient pacing and the pleasure of learning a landscape on foot. We leave space for questions, quiet stretches and a second look.

WHAT WE WOULD BRING TO EVERY DAY

Good company.
Thoughtful choices.

Small by intention

The sample group size is up to six walkers. Small enough to find a shared pace, hear one another and keep the day personal.

Grounded in place

Stay on established paths, respect working land and give wildlife room. Taking care of the outdoors is part of enjoying it.

Flexible by nature

The summit is an option, not an obligation. Conditions and the group’s comfort guide the shape of the day.

A few things to know

Do I need walking experience?

The gentle day walks are a good starting point. Ridge routes suit regular hill walkers. Share your experience and any access needs before choosing a route.

What if the weather changes?

The sample plan includes a sheltered alternative. In real trip planning, route changes, postponements and turnaround decisions would be discussed before departure.

Can I come on my own?

Yes, the small-group concept is designed with solo walkers in mind. Private guiding is another option for a day shaped around your own pace.

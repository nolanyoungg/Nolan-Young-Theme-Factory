A service website structured around better decisions.

The challenge is common: important services are spread across unclear pages, visitors do not know where to begin, and the owner needs a site that is easier to update.

Challenge
Simplify a complex service offer without hiding necessary detail.
Solution
Reusable service cards, guided CTAs, article previews, and private inquiry records.
Outcome

Proof without invented endorsements

Credibility comes from the process, structure, and quality controls built into the theme.

  • Private form and newsletter records stay out of public archives.
  • The header, drawer, filters, and accordions use ARIA state changes and visible focus styles.
  • Compiled assets are generated from source SCSS and JavaScript with local paths.

02.1 / Residential architecture

A home, in your own terms.

An engagement shaped around the site and the life you want to make there. New homes, thoughtful extensions and careful transformations of existing places.

The engagement

From daily rituals
to a clear direction.

We begin with what matters to you: somewhere to gather, a quiet place to work, an easier relationship with the garden. Those ordinary needs give the design its purpose.

Early work considers the site, existing fabric, planning context and budget priorities. We then test a small number of spatial approaches before developing one together.

Later technical design, consultant coordination and construction-stage services would be set out in an agreed written scope. Fees and timing depend on the project; there is no fixed package or implied approval.

Before we begin

A few useful
questions.

Do I need a finished brief?

No. Start with the location, the spaces you need and what is not working today. We would develop a clear brief together before design work begins.

Can you work with an existing building?

Existing buildings are central to our approach. The first step would be to understand the structure, condition and constraints, with specialist surveys where appropriate.

How are fees and timing agreed?

A real engagement would begin with a written proposal defining scope, deliverables, fees and review stages. Timing would reflect the size of the project, approvals and consultant input. This sample site does not quote or book services.

Can interiors be a separate commission?

Yes, the sample service covers stand-alone interior planning, joinery and material selection as well as interiors developed alongside an architectural scheme.

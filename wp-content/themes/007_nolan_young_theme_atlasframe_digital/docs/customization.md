# Customization

Customize global colors, spacing, typography, component styling, header behavior, footer layout, and page-specific styling in `src/scss/`. JavaScript interactions live in `src/js/main.js`. The compiled files in `assets/css/bundle.css` and `assets/js/bundle.js` are generated outputs and must keep their required names.

Business copy lives in templates and helper data arrays. Add only verified Atlasframe Digital details, approved local images, and WordPress-managed legal content.
